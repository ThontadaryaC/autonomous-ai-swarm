# 🌍 Daily Intelligence & Live News Engine

This skill keeps your agents completely up-to-date with live world events and reports on daily skill evolution.

---

## 🌟 Daily Capabilities

1. **🧠 Skill Updates (`what are the new updates in your skills?`)**:
   - Reports all new rules, patterns, and playbooks learned today.
   - Shows current level progression across backend, frontend, security, and QA skills.

2. **🇺🇸 USA News (`what is the most interesting news in USA?`)**:
   - Live top headlines across US news, politics, and culture.
   - Live trending technology & AI updates in the USA.

3. **🇮🇳 India News (`what is the most interesting news in India?`)**:
   - Live top national headlines across India.
   - Trending Indian technology, startups, and innovation news.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

- **All-in-one Daily Briefing:**
  ```powershell
  python c:\Users\cool\Desktop\skills\daily_intelligence.py
  ```

- **Ask USA News only:**
  ```powershell
  python c:\Users\cool\Desktop\skills\daily_intelligence.py "USA"
  ```

- **Ask India News only:**
  ```powershell
  python c:\Users\cool\Desktop\skills\daily_intelligence.py "India"
  ```

- **Ask Skill Updates only:**
  ```powershell
  python c:\Users\cool\Desktop\skills\daily_intelligence.py "skills"
  ```
